## 定义

记 $(a)_n := a (a+1) \cdots (a+n-1)$ 为上阶乘. **超几何函数**是如下形式幂级数的和:
$$
{_p}F{_q}\Big(
    {{a_1,\cdots,a_p}\atop {b_1,\cdots,b_q}};z
\Big):=
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\frac{
    (a_1)_n\cdots (a_p)_n
}{
    (b_1)_n\cdots (b_q)_n
}\frac{z^n}{n!}.
$$
